⚠️ CHALLENGE TIME ⚠️

🏆 چالش شما

⏱️ You have 5 minutes to design and prepare a short Kids English Activity.
یک موضوع ساده انتخاب کنید: 🐶 Animals · 🍎 Food · 🎨 Colors · 🧸 Toys · 🏃🏻 Actions

Your Activity Must Include:

  • 1️⃣ Learning Objective — دقیقاً چه چیزی یاد گرفته شود؟
  • 2️⃣ Student Interaction — Pair / Group / Whole-class
  • 3️⃣ Movement — حرکت و مشارکت بدنی
  • 4️⃣ Short & Clear Instructions — دستورهای دقیق آماده باشد
  • 5️⃣ Kids-Appropriate — متناسب با سن و سطح
  • 6️⃣ STT — بچه‌ها فعالانه انگلیسی بگویند
  • 7️⃣ Fun + Purpose — سرگرم‌کننده + تعاملی + مؤثر
FINAL ROUND

🎤 Now, Teach It!

⏱️ You have 2–3 minutes to demonstrate your Activity as if you were teaching a real Kids class.

👩🏻‍🏫 One Teacher → Teacher 👧🏻👦🏻 Other Teachers → Students

▶️ Start your Activity · Give instructions · Demonstrate · Monitor · Interact
🔄 Then switch roles!
